# Covers and visual sources / 封面与素材来源

[中文首页](../README.md) · [English home](../README.en.md)

The covers use deep indigo on pale paper, with a restrained amber route linking three reading stages. Nine filled waypoints represent the handbook’s nine chapters: two for getting started, four for developing the research, and three for collaboration and communication. The route is a conceptual reading map, not a chart of research results.

封面以靛蓝、浅纸白与少量琥珀色表现阅读路线。九个实心圆点对应九章内容：开始工作两章、推进研究四章、协作与汇报三章。路线是章节组织的概念示意，不表示研究结果或实测数据。

The chapter structure follows the public handbook. The covers contain no real manuscript content, author identities, experimental results, or external photography. English uses regular book-serif typography; Chinese uses regular Songti with natural character proportions.

章节结构依据公开手册。封面不包含真实稿件内容、作者身份、实验结果或外部摄影素材。英文使用常规书籍衬线字形，中文使用字形规整的宋体。
